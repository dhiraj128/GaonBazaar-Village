Privacy Policy (गोपनीयता नीति)

Last Updated & Effective: November 2026 • Version 2.4

1. Overview & Commitment / परिचय और उद्देश्य

Apna GaonBazaar ("GaonBazaar", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to providing high-speed rural hyperlocal delivery services across village panchayats in India. We value your trust and are strictly committed to safeguarding your personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, the Information Technology Act 2000, and Google Play Store User Data Policies.

2. Information We Collect / हम कौन सी जानकारी एकत्र करते हैं

  • Personal Identification Data: Mobile phone number, full name, and preferred communication language. Mobile verification is conducted securely via transactional SMS OTP (2Factor.in).
  • Delivery Addresses & Geolocation: Village name, panchayat, landmark, and GPS coordinates strictly used for verifying delivery eligibility (within our 9.0 km pilot coverage radius) and routing assigned local runners.
  • Partner Verification Data (Vendors & Runners only): Shop registration name, government-issued photo identity (offline UIDAI Secure QR / PAN) and verified UPI / bank payout details for direct settlements. Customers are NEVER required to submit government IDs.
  • Transaction & Order History: Product items ordered, quantities, payment method (UPI Intent / Cash on Delivery), and delivery timestamps.

3. How We Use Your Data / डेटा का उपयोग

  • To process and deliver grocery, farm-fresh, and essential orders to rural households within 15–30 minutes.
  • To route delivery runners efficiently across rural unpaved roads and local landmarks.
  • To provide bilingual in-app notifications and transactional delivery status updates.
  • To settle daily merchant disbursements and runner earnings transparently (80% runner delivery fee share).
  • Strict Prohibition: We NEVER sell, lease, or monetize your personal data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Security & Storage / डेटा सुरक्षा

All network communications between user devices and our backend APIs are encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3 / HTTPS. Session authentication uses RFC 7519 cryptographic HS256 tokens stored with secure HttpOnly cookie attributes. Sensitive database entries are shielded by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion / खाता और डेटा हटाना

In compliance with Google Play Store policies, users have full control over their account data. You can delete your account and personal data at any time:

  • In-App: Go to Account / Profile → Settings → "Delete Account".
  • Online Portal: Submit an instantaneous data erasure request via our public web portal at apnagaonbazaar.in/delete-account.

Upon deletion, your profile details, mobile number, avatar, and saved delivery addresses are immediately anonymized and purged. Anonymized tax and statutory invoicing records are retained strictly as required by applicable Indian commercial regulations.
