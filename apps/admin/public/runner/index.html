Help & Operations
Delivery Partner Guidelines & Operations FAQ
5 Points
1. What are the delivery radius & cluster geofencing rules?
Gaon Mitra operates within an 8km geofenced village cluster. Missions are assigned close to your base village to ensure 15–30 minute doorsteps while keeping bike fuel expenditure low.
2. How should I handle unreachable customers at the doorstep?
If a customer is unreachable, attempt at least 3 phone calls within 5 minutes. If still unavailable, notify your Hub Manager in the app. Never complete delivery without the customer's 4-digit handover OTP.
3. How and when do I deposit daily COD cash at the rural hub?
All cash collected from Cash on Delivery (COD) orders must be deposited at the Muzaffarpur rural operations hub at the end of each evening shift. The Hub Manager will instantly reconcile your cash collection.
4. What is the weekly delivery payout schedule?
Delivery fees and completed order bonuses are settled directly to your verified bank account or UPI ID every Friday night. You can review all completed trip earnings in real-time in your Earnings tab.
5. Why is my delivery payout slightly less on online orders?
For online payments, backend processors like Razorpay charge a standard fee to process the electronic transfer. Cash on Delivery (COD) deliveries do not have this online fee.
