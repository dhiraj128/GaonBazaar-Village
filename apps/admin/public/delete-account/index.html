Account & Data Deletion Request (खाता और डेटा हटाने का अनुरोध)

Apna GaonBazaar Customer, Vendor & Runner Applications

1. Your Right to Delete Data / डेटा हटाने का अधिकार

Under Google Play's User Data policy and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, you have the absolute right to request the deletion of your Apna GaonBazaar account and all associated personal records.

2. What Happens When You Delete Your Account / क्या डेटा हटाया जाता है

Permanently Deleted / Anonymized:

  • Your profile details (Full name, phone number, email address)
  • Profile avatars and uploaded KYC documents/photos
  • Saved delivery locations, village landmarks, and GPS pins
  • Active authentication session tokens (instant revocation)

Retained for Statutory Compliance:

  • Completed financial invoice receipts (retained strictly in anonymized form per Indian tax laws for 7 years)
  • Fraud prevention transaction IDs (non-PII)

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instantaneous)

  1. Open the Apna GaonBazaar app on your Android phone.
  2. Navigate to the Account or Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete My Account".
  4. Confirm the prompt. Your personal profile and saved addresses are purged immediately.

Method 2: Online Deletion Request Form

Submit this form if you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your phone.
