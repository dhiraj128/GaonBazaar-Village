Account & Data Deletion (खाता और डेटा हटाने का अनुरोध)

Apna GaonBazaar Customer, Vendor & Runner Applications

1. Who Can Request Account Deletion / कौन अनुरोध कर सकता है

Any registered user of Apna GaonBazaar across all three applications may request complete deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time:

  • Customers (ग्राहक): App users who order groceries and farm produce.
  • Vendors (दुकानदार): Village shopkeepers and merchants listing store products.
  • Runners (डिलीवरी पार्टनर): Village delivery partners fulfilling order deliveries.

2. What Data is Deleted vs. Retained / डेटा विलोपन विवरण

Permanently Deleted / Anonymized:
  • Your personal profile (Full name, mobile number, email, avatar photo)
  • Saved delivery locations, village landmarks, and GPS pins
  • KYC documents, Aadhaar metadata, and PAN records
  • Active authentication session tokens (instant revocation across all devices)
  • Connected vendor catalog or runner driver profiles (marked permanently inactive)
Retained for Statutory Compliance:
  • Completed financial transaction receipts and tax invoices (retained strictly in anonymized, non-PII format per Indian GST & Companies Act rules for 7 years)
  • Fraud prevention and anti-money laundering reference records (non-PII)

3. Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instantaneous)

If you have the Apna GaonBazaar app installed on your Android smartphone, in-app deletion is instantaneous:

  1. Open the Apna GaonBazaar (Customer, Dukaan, or Mitra) app.
  2. Navigate to the Account or Dashboard tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account & Data" (खाता और डेटा हटाएं).
  4. Confirm the confirmation prompt. Your profile and personal records are purged immediately and sessions revoked.

5. How Account Ownership is Verified / पहचान सत्यापन

To prevent unauthorized or fraudulent deletion of accounts by third parties, online requests submitted through this webpage undergo secure verification:

  • An automated confirmation SMS is dispatched to your registered Indian mobile number.
  • If an active order or delivery is currently in transit, deletion is paused until the ongoing order is safely delivered or cancelled.
  • For vendor stores with pending settlement balances, payouts must clear before shop profile closure.

6. Processing Timeline / प्रक्रिया की समय-सीमा

  • In-App Deletion: Immediate (0 to 60 seconds).
  • Online Web Requests: Processed within 24 to 48 hours following mobile verification per the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.
