Refund & Replacement Policy

1. 180-Second Cancellation Window

Customers can cancel an active order within 180 seconds (3 minutes) of placement with zero cancellation fee. The refund is instantaneous for prepaid online orders and immediate for COD orders.

2. Missing, Damaged & Wrong Items

If any item is missing, damaged, or wrong upon delivery, report the issue within 2 hours of delivery via the Orders tab or AI Support Chat. We offer free immediate replacement from the nearest store or a full refund.

3. Refund Processing & Timelines

• Cash on Delivery: Instant credit or cash adjustment upon inspection.
• Online UPI / Cards: 5–7 business days settlement through the banking provider. We provide genuine gateway refund reference IDs.

4. Perishables & Hygiene Items

Fresh milk, green vegetables, and sealed sanitary goods can be inspected at the time of delivery before OTP verification. Once accepted with OTP, perishables cannot be returned unless damaged during transit.
