2. Hyperlocal Serviceability & Coverage / सेवा क्षेत्र

Our pilot fulfillment service operates within a strict 9.0 km radius of the central pilot hub (Minapur / Muzaffarpur, Bihar). Delivery addresses situated outside this geofenced boundary are automatically notified of non-serviceability to prevent unfulfilled promises.

3. Transparent Rural Pricing & Platform Fee / पारदर्शी शुल्क नीति

  • Checkout Platform Fee: A flat, nominal platform convenience fee of ₹3.00 applies to all customer orders.
  • Distance-Based Delivery Fee: Tiered transparently by GPS road distance:
    • 0.0 – 2.0 km: ₹15.00
    • 2.0 – 4.0 km: ₹20.00
    • 4.0 – 6.0 km: ₹25.00
    • 6.0 – 8.0 km: ₹35.00
    • 8.0 – 9.0 km: ₹40.00
  • Fair Runner Payout: Exactly 80% of the customer delivery fee (e.g. ₹12 to ₹32 per order) is disbursed directly to the local runner partner.
  • 0% Merchant Commission: Local village shopkeepers pay zero listing or commission fees during introductory pilot operations.

4. Order Cancellation Policy / रद्दीकरण नियम

Customers may cancel an order free of charge within 180 seconds (3 minutes) of placement. After this grace window, merchant preparation and runner dispatch begin, and cancellation is restricted to protect local shopkeeper inventory and runner fuel costs.

5. Returns, Replacements & Refunds / रिफंड और वापसी नीति

Damaged, missing, or expired grocery items reported with photographic proof via our Help Desk within 24 hours of delivery are eligible for an immediate replacement delivery or a 100% refund credited back to the original UPI / payment source within 2–4 business hours.

6. User Conduct & Security / सुरक्षा नियम

Users must provide accurate mobile numbers during OTP authentication. Abusive behavior toward delivery runners or fraudulent order placement will result in permanent account suspension.
