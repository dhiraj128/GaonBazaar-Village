Connecting Villages with Local Kirana Stores

Apna GaonBazaar connects rural households with authorized local village stores and verified delivery partners (Gaon Mitra) for fast, honest doorstep delivery.

  • Daily Essentials & Groceries: Atta, dal, oil, spices, fresh produce, and household goods from nearby village shops.
  • Local Store Partners: Supporting local rural retailers through the GaonBazaar Dukaan platform.
  • Delivery Partners: Gaon Mitra runners providing reliable last-mile village delivery.
